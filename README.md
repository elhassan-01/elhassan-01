## 👋 Hi, I'm Dr. Elhassan Mohamed

I am a **Research Associate in Safe, Explainable and Trustworthy AI at The Alan Turing Institute**, where I work on **Project Bluebird**, developing an expert machine learning system for air traffic control that is interpretable and explainable by design. The aim is an AI agent that can tell the controllers who work alongside it *what* it recommends and *why*.

Before that, my PhD at the **University of Kent** focused on computer vision and deep learning for assistive robotics. I built systems that help visually impaired powered-wheelchair users understand their surroundings by classifying every pixel of the scene, and I took them off the workstation and onto a GPU-based board on a real wheelchair for practical evaluation.

Across all of it, one question keeps coming back: **how do we make a black-box model justify its predictions well enough to be trusted in the real world?**

## 🚀 Current focus: Transparent AI agents for air traffic control

Air traffic control is a safety-critical setting, so an agent that only outputs an action is not enough. Controllers need to see the reasoning behind it. Within Project Bluebird (a collaboration between NATS, The Alan Turing Institute and the University of Exeter) my work centres on:

- Machine learning agents that are **interpretable and explainable by design**, not explained after the fact
- **Digital twins** of airspace as a place to develop, test and trust these agents
- Human-in-the-loop AI that acts as a reliable partner to the operator rather than a replacement

📄 *Towards Transparent AI Agents for Air Traffic Control*, presented at the AIAA SciTech 2026 Forum, Orlando (Jan 2026), co-authored with Ben Carvell, Rob Procter, Eseoghene Benjamin, George De Ath and Richard Everson: [published, peer-reviewed version](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/195367/) · [SSRN preprint](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6042354)

## 🔬 Research themes

### 🔍 Explainable & trustworthy AI
Making deep networks transparent enough for real applications: which features drive a prediction, where the dataset is biased, and where the architecture is weak.
- [A review of visualisation-as-explanation techniques for CNNs and their evaluation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S014193822200066X), *Displays* (2022)
- [Optimisation of deep learning small-object detectors with novel explainable verification](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/362286228_Optimisation_of_Deep_Learning_Small-Object_Detectors_with_Novel_Explainable_Verification), *Sensors* (2022)

### 👁️ Computer vision for assistive technology
Real-time scene understanding for powered-wheelchair users: semantic segmentation, object detection and distance estimation, deployed on embedded GPU hardware.
- [Indoor/Outdoor semantic segmentation using deep learning for visually impaired wheelchair users](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3123952), *IEEE Access* (2021)
- [Real-time powered wheelchair assistive navigation system based on intelligent semantic segmentation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/368911136_Real-time_Powered_Wheelchair_Assistive_Navigation_System_Based_on_Intelligent_Semantic_Segmentation_for_Visually_Impaired_Users) (2022)

### 🗂️ Datasets & evaluation
Application-specific data is scarce, so I build it, and I look for better ways to judge the systems trained on it.
- [A pixel-wise annotated dataset of small overlooked indoor objects](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/357585739_A_pixel-wise_annotated_dataset_of_small_overlooked_indoor_objects_for_semantic_segmentation_applications), *Data in Brief* (2022)
- [Multi-environment object detection dataset for indoor navigation: door handle types](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372437187_Multi-Environment_Object_Detection_Dataset_for_Improved_Indoor_Navigation_Focusing_on_the_Importance_of_Different_Door_Handle_Types) (2023)
- [Incorporation of rejection criterion: a novel technique for evaluating semantic segmentation systems](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354672109_Incorporation_Of_Rejection_Criterion_-_A_Novel_Technique_For_Evaluating_Semantic_Segmentation_Systems) (2021)

### 📡 Sensing, vibration & user comfort
How terrain-induced vibration affects both the user (ISO 2631 whole-body vibration) and the vision systems mounted on the moving platform.
- [Investigation of vibration and user comfort for electrical powered wheelchairs](https://doi.org/10.1109/LSENS.2022.3147740), *IEEE Sensors Letters* (2022)
- [Analysing the impact of vibrations on smart wheelchair systems and users](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/361002645_Analysing_the_Impact_of_Vibrations_on_Smart_Wheelchair_Systems_and_Users), *LNCS* (2022)

### 🩺 AI for health & rehabilitation
- Smart gait prediction system for the rehabilitation of children with cerebral palsy (Canterbury Christ Church University)
- Remote, computer-vision-based measurement of chewing efficiency (University of Kent, School of Computing)

## 🌱 Currently exploring

- **Multipurpose neural networks**: one model trained to perform several related tasks (for example indoor and outdoor scene understanding) without retraining on a merged dataset
- **Data-centric and lifelong learning**: models that improve as new data arrives, and how to decide which new data actually helps
- **Robustness and supervision**: automatic weight updates can be exploited by attackers, so continually learning systems need oversight to stay reliable
- **Multimodal understanding** and human-in-the-loop AI for decision support
